title: Three peoples, rethought
date: 2026-11-01
summary: The five peoples are retired. Galeborne, Anchorkin and Rootwardens replace them, each with an innate skill only they can learn.
---

The first lineup had too many beast-folk, and it was hard to say who you would want to be. So we started again from the player fantasy and kept the cast small: three peoples at launch, with room for more later.

## The peoples

- **Galeborne**: hollow-boned, feather-mantled folk of the Crag Spires, the highest isles. Their innate skill is **Glide**: they glide down from any height and ride wind currents between nearby isles without a ship.
- **Anchorkin**: huge stone-skinned folk of the Moorings, the lowest isles, which are chained together above the storm. Their innate skill is **Anchor**: nothing knocks them back, and they can steady a ship or hold a breaking bridge.
- **Rootwardens**: the bark-skinned tree folk of the Rootholds. Their innate skill is **Take Root**: they stand still to regenerate, and help allies regenerate near an ancient tree.

Slowing an isle's sinking is now **Treetending**, a skill anyone can learn. The Rootwardens are simply the best at it.

## Grudges

The Galeborne need ancient wood for their ships, and the Rootwardens guard it, so Grudgewood is still the oldest quarrel on the shard. The Rootwardens and Anchorkin are old allies who hold the land. The Galeborne and Anchorkin trade, but the Anchorkin don't trust a people who can glide away from any isle the moment it starts to fall.

The Garden Isles in the east now belong to no one. They are farmland for player plots, and they're kept free for the next people.

The old concept art and models are kept as drafts. Lanternborn and Oathbound are on the shelf for later.
