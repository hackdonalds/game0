The isles float above the drowned old world, and nobody knows why. What everyone does know is that the isles are sinking. Every so often one loses its lift and settles into the storm layer below, so land is shrinking and every isle is worth fighting for.

## The peoples

Five peoples share the skies under the **Tether Accord**, a fragile trade pact that keeps the towns neutral and the sky lanes open:

- **Rootborn** tend the ancient trees in the Rootholds.
- **Veiled Ones** wander in mourning for their sunken city, Vael.
- **Skerrin** mine the Crag Spires.
- **Mossbacks** keep the Ledger of every sunken isle.
- **Rillfolk** trade with everyone and dive into the drowned world.

Old grudges run under the Accord. The Rootborn blame Skerrin mining for the sinking, and the Veiled resent the Rillfolk for robbing Vael's dead.

## Customization

Every people has a base body, signature features and props that attach to the skeleton: Skerrin horns, Veiled masks, Mossback shell gardens, Rootborn growths and Rillfolk tail rings.

## How you'll play

There are no classes. About 35 skills grow as you use them, and you choose six learned techniques at a time. Accord towns are safe, the sky lanes are guarded, and the wild isles are open PvP, where you drop what you carry if you fall.
