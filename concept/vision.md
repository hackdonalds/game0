# Vision

## Pitch

An epic high-fantasy sandbox MMORPG set across floating sky isles. Players aren't locked into a role: anyone can settle, adventure, craft, trade or fight, and switch between them by what they learn and carry rather than by what they picked at character creation.

## Pillars

1. **Play any path.** Classless skills and open professions. Your weapon, your learned skills and your gear define your role, and you can change it at any time.
2. **Combat is skill.** Action combat: aim, dodge, positioning and combos matter more than gear score.
3. **Risk feeds the world.** Open-world PvP with safe havens, and partial loot on death. What gets lost flows back into gathering, crafting and trade.
4. **Epic, luminous fantasy.** Grand vistas, ancient powers and real conflict, with a hopeful tone (see [art direction](art-direction/README.md)).
5. **Shards with a community feel.** Small persistent worlds where players recognise each other, so reputation, rivalry and guilds carry weight.

## Decisions so far

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Player role | Free choice of settling, adventuring, crafting, fighting and trading |
| Progression | Classless, use-based skills: skills rise by use under a total skill cap that forces specialisation |
| Professions | Open to everyone, no lock-in |
| Combat | Action, skill-based |
| Tone | Epic high fantasy |
| PvP | Open world, with safe havens (towns and sanctuaries) |
| Death | Partial loot drop: carried resources drop, equipped gear is kept (and loses durability) |
| Scale | Small shards, about 50–200 concurrent players per world |
| Travel | Bridges and paths between nearby isles; crafted player airships for longer routes and cargo |
| Housing | Personal plots in safe towns (cosmetics and crafting stations); no guild land claims for now |
| World premise | Open, to be decided in the world session |

## Open questions

- What is the total skill cap, and how do skills decay or get lowered to make room?
- Which zones count as safe, and how does the PvP risk gradient work across isles?
- How many town plots per shard, and how are they allocated?
- Can airships be attacked or boarded, and what happens to their cargo?
- Is there persistence across shards (characters or markets), or is each shard sealed?

## Target players

Mid-core sandbox fans: players who enjoy Albion Online or New World, play a few sessions a week, and value the economy, crafting and guild play over raid schedules.

## Comparable games

Albion Online (classless, loot economy), New World (action combat, professions), Ultima Online (sandbox freedom), World of Warcraft (profession breadth, world tone).
