# Glossary

Short definitions of the words used across the concept docs, grouped by the handbook chapter they belong to. The handbook shows them as its [glossary](https://hackdonalds.github.io/game0/catalogue/glossary/).

Write a definition here only when no doc or entry already says it. When one does, link to it instead (`- **Term**: [source](path)`): the handbook then shows the first paragraph of that doc section, or the summary of that entry, so the words live in one place.

## Vision

- **Living world**: No final boss and no endgame: the world keeps moving, with isles sinking and being born, NPCs living their lives and the seasons turning.
- **Shard**: A small persistent world for about 50 to 200 players. Characters and markets are shared across shards, and players can move between them.

## The World

- **Accord Hall**: [Accord Hall](entries/isles/isle_accord-hall.md)
- **Accord towns**: Neutral, safe towns kept open by the Tether Accord. The world file marks which isles of a shard are Accord towns.
- **Ancient trees**: Huge old trees on some isles. Treetending these trees slows an isle's sinking, and the Rootwardens tend them best.
- **Dive**: A high-risk trip below the clouds into the drowned world to recover relics with the Salvage skill.
- **Drowned world**: Everything below the clouds and the storm layer: the ruined lands and cities of the old world.
- **First Shard**: The first world map: Accord Hall at the centre, Accord towns between the homelands, and a wild rim.
- **Garden Isles**: The eastern isles of the First Shard. They belong to no people: terraced farmland open for player plots, and room for a future people.
- **Isle birth**: A rare world event in which a new isle rises out of the storm layer. The whole shard races to chart, settle and hold it.
- **Sinking**: An isle losing its lift and slowly settling into the storm layer. Sinking can be slowed by Treetending but never stopped once it starts.
- **Storm layer**: The permanent living storm below the isles. It is slowly rising, sends creatures up and pulls isles down.
- **Tether Accord**: [The Tether Accord](world/README.md#the-tether-accord)
- **The Moorings**: The Anchorkin homeland in the south of the First Shard: the lowest isles, chained together near the storm. Its shrine isle is Ledgerstone.
- **Treetending**: [Treetending](entries/skills/treetending.md)
- **Vael**: The greatest city of the drowned old world. Its ruins lie below Vael's Shadow, and what sank it is still a mystery.

## Peoples

- **Affinity**: A skill a people is culturally known for. Affinities never restrict anyone: any people can learn any skill.
- **Anchor**: [Anchor](entries/skills/anchor.md)
- **Anchorkin**: [Anchorkin](entries/peoples/anchorkin.md)
- **Galeborne**: [Galeborne](entries/peoples/galeborne.md)
- **Glide**: [Glide](entries/skills/glide.md)
- **Innate skill**: The one skill each people has that no other people can learn. It rises with use like any other skill.
- **Ledger**: The Anchorkin record, carved in stone at Ledgerstone, of every isle that has ever sunk and why. Its entry for Vael is on a broken stone.
- **Rootwardens**: [Rootwardens](entries/peoples/rootwarden.md)
- **Take Root**: [Take Root](entries/skills/take-root.md)

## How You Play

- **Accord shrine**: A shrine where characters respawn after death. You return at the nearest one.
- **Achievements**: A record of what a character has done. With quests, they give direction in a world with no endgame.
- **Cooldown**: The wait before a strong technique can be used again. Cooldowns and stamina are the only limits on techniques.
- **Decay**: Unused skills slowly fall, but never below half their peak, so taking an old skill up again is quick. There is no cap on total skill.
- **Guarded**: The middle risk tier: Accord lanes and each people's home isles. PvP is consensual only, and death costs durability only.
- **Job board**: A town board of repeatable jobs from NPCs and players, such as hauling, gathering, hunting and escorting.
- **Oathcraft**: [Oathcraft](entries/skills/oathcraft.md)
- **Partial loot**: On death in wild space, the resources you carry drop where you fell. Your equipped gear stays with you.
- **Risk tiers**: The three kinds of space: safe, guarded and wild. Each sets the PvP rules and what death costs.
- **Safe**: The lowest risk tier: the Accord towns. There is no PvP.
- **Sinking-isle event**: An isle's last days on a shard. Players evacuate settlers, salvage relics and fight for the spoils before it falls.
- **Skill**: Everything a character can do, from swordplay to Wind casting to cooking. Each skill runs from 0 to 100 and rises with use.
- **Source**: Where a skill is learned: a trainer, a book, or a place tied to the skill.
- **Stamina**: The single bar used for everything: swinging, casting, dodging and sprinting. It refills quickly at rest, and there is no mana.
- **Technique**: A learned ability unlocked at skill 25, 50, 75 or 100. Techniques come from trainers, books, world bosses and relic finds.
- **Technique slots**: The six places where you set the techniques you can use. Slots can be swapped at any time outside combat.
- **Trainer**: A teacher of a skill and its techniques. Any player who reaches 75 in a skill can become a trainer.
- **Wild**: The highest risk tier: contested, wild and sinking isles and uncharted routes. PvP is open and carried resources drop on death.

## Sailing

- **Accord lanes**: The charted, steady wind currents that link towns and homelands. They run through guarded space, so PvP on them is consensual only.
- **Ambient wind**: The light wind that blows across the whole shard outside the wind currents.
- **Galleon**: [Galleon](entries/ships/galleon.md)
- **Helmsman**: The galleon crew member who steers the ship and chooses its heading.
- **Sail wear**: Sails wear from attacks, weather and distance travelled. Worn sails catch less wind until a Sailweaver re-enchants them.
- **Sailmaster**: The galleon crew member who works the sails while the helmsman steers.
- **Wild currents**: Fast, gusty and shifting wind currents through open-PvP sky: shortcuts with risk.

## Economy and Crafting

- **Durability**: How worn a piece of gear is. Gear wears with use and on death, and crafters repair or replace it.
- **Gear tier**: The strength band of a piece of gear, set by its material: copper, iron, skysteel or relic. Crafting skill sets its quality within that tier.
- **Gold sink**: Anything that takes gold or items out of the world, such as repairs, plot rent, sail wear and resources lost on death.
- **Order book**: A town's local market of buy and sell orders. Each town's book is shared across shards, but goods must still be hauled there.
- **Quality**: How good an item is within its tier. The crafter's skill sets the quality roll.
- **Relic**: An object or metal from the old world, found in dives, ruins, sinking isles and world bosses. Relics are identified with Lore.
- **Skill book**: A book that teaches a skill or technique. Players at skill 100 can write them, and they are traded like goods.
- **Town plot**: A personal plot in a safe town, rented weekly with gold. It holds a house, crafting stations, storage and a dock.

## Creatures

- **Danger mark**: A sign that shows how strong a creature is compared to your skills. Together with size and look, it keeps danger readable.
- **Flying mount**: A rare tamed creature that carries one rider through the air. Flying mounts tire and cannot replace ships.
- **Population**: The number of each creature an isle holds. It refills over time, and overhunting thins it out for a while.
- **Storm surge**: A burst of the storm's power at a wild isle. Surges bring stormspawn and world bosses, and they are the only surprise in creature danger.
- **Storm-touched**: A creature twisted by the storm below. Storm-touched creatures are more dangerous and always hostile.
- **Temperament**: How a creature reacts to you: passive, neutral, territorial or hostile.
- **World boss**: A huge creature that rises from the storm during a surge. It scales with the number of players nearby, and everyone who helps gets a reward.

## Society

- **Grudges**: Old quarrels between peoples, such as Rootwardens against Galeborne over felling the ancient trees. They give PvP its flavour without hard factions.

## Simulation

- **Abstract level**: The lightest level of NPC simulation. A far-away character follows a plan with a start and end time, and its position is only worked out when someone needs it.
- **Bot character**: A server-side character that plays by the same rules as players: it gathers, crafts, hauls, trades and fights. Bots feed the real economy and thin out as real players join.

## Art Direction

- **Painterly style**: The game's stylised, hand-painted look, with soft lighting and gentle rim light. It is grand and luminous, never grim.
