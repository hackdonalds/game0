# World

> Approved 2026-11-01.

- [Peoples](peoples.md): the three playable peoples

## Premise

A world of floating isles above the **drowned old world**. Nobody knows why the isles float: scholars, priests and expeditions all disagree, and that mystery drives exploration. Below the clouds and a permanent storm layer lie the ruins of the civilisation that came before.

**The isles are sinking.** Every so often an isle loses its lift and slowly settles into the storm layer and the drowned world below. Land is shrinking, so claiming and holding isles is what everyone fights over.

**Sinking can be slowed, never stopped.** Tending the ancient trees (the Treetending skill, a Rootwarden speciality) slows an isle's sinking, but once an isle starts to fall it can't be saved.

**New isles are born.** Rarely, a new isle rises out of the storm layer. An isle's birth is a world event: unclaimed land, untouched resource nodes and a race to settle and hold it.

**The storm below is alive.** The storm layer is slowly rising. It sends creatures up onto the isles and pulls isles down. It never wins and is never defeated: it is the constant pressure that makes the peoples need each other, and the reason the Tether Accord holds.

## Tone and technology

Pure high fantasy: swords, bows, spells and craft. No guns or engines. Ships are wooden sailships carried by enchanted sails and wind currents. They are common and player-crafted, as the [economy](../economy/README.md) assumes.

## The Tether Accord

A fragile trade pact between the three peoples, sworn at Oathgate after a great sinking that nearly broke every people at once. It keeps the sky lanes open and the towns neutral. Under it, old grudges still run deep, and open-world PvP lives in the gaps between Accord towns.

## Homelands

| People | Homeland | Role in the world |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Galeborne** | **The Crag Spires**: sheer cliff isles, the highest on the shard | Sailors and shipwrights; their couriers link every town |
| **Anchorkin** | **The Moorings**: the lowest isles, chained together near the storm | Keepers of the **Ledger**, the record in stone of every isle that has sunk |
| **Rootwardens** | **The Rootholds**: caverns in the roots beneath the oldest isles | Tend the ancient trees, which slows the sinking |

The **Garden Isles** in the east belong to no people: terraced farmland open for player plots, and room for a future people.

## Old grudges and ties

- **Galeborne vs Rootwardens:** the Galeborne need ancient wood for masts and hulls; the Rootwardens say every old tree felled hastens the sinking.
- **Rootwardens and Anchorkin:** old allies who hold the land, one with roots and the other with chains.
- **Galeborne and Anchorkin:** wary trade partners. The Anchorkin don't trust a people who can leave any isle the moment it starts to fall.

## The First Shard

Map: [`worlds/first-shard.world.json`](../../worlds/first-shard.world.json), editable in the world editor demo.

- **Accord Hall** sits at the centre. Four Accord towns (**Oathgate**, **Tetherport**, **Windmarket**, **Lanterncross**) sit between the homelands, joined by the clockwise **Accord Ring** wind lane, with spokes to the hub.
- **Homelands**: the Rootholds in the north, the Garden Isles in the east, the Moorings in the south (lowest, nearest the storm) and the Crag Spires in the west (highest). Each has a shrine isle bridged to three smaller isles and a lane from the nearest town.
- **Neighbours follow the ties**: the Galeborne border both other homelands, as traders and rivals; the Rootholds and Crag Spires share the contested northwest frontier. The Garden Isles in the east are unclaimed farmland.
- **Wild rim**: **Grudgewood**, an ancient tree whose wood makes the finest masts, on the northwest frontier, between the Rootholds and the Crag Spires and beyond Oathgate; **Vael's Shadow**, ruins above Vael, the greatest city of the drowned old world; **Ashfall** and **Wreckrest** are sinking. Three wild currents (**The Long Gale**, **Drowned Drift**, **Grudge Wind**) are fast shortcuts through open-PvP sky.

## Gameplay hooks

- **Isle births** are rare world events: the whole shard races to chart, settle and contest the new land.
- **Sinking isles** can be a live event: an isle's last days, with evacuation, salvage and a race for its resources.
- **Drowned-world dives** are high-risk, high-reward expeditions for old-world relics, using the Salvage skill. Any player can dive, though the Anchorkin are too heavy to swim and must go down by rope and chain.
- **Grudges** give PvP its flavour without hard factions; the Accord towns are the safe havens.

## Time and seasons

The world has a day and night cycle and four seasons.

## Open questions

- What the storm is: a force of nature, something sleeping, or the old world's doing? Players may never fully learn.
- How storm surges play out: creature waves, faster sinking, blocked wind lanes.
- What sank Vael? The Ledger has an entry for it, but the stone is broken. That could be a long-term mystery.
