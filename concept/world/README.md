# World

> Approved 2026-11-01.

- [Peoples](peoples.md): the five playable peoples

## Premise

A world of floating isles above the **drowned old world**. Nobody knows why the isles float: scholars, priests and expeditions all disagree, and that mystery drives exploration. Below the clouds and a permanent storm layer lie the ruins of the civilisation that came before.

**The isles are sinking.** Every so often an isle loses its lift and slowly settles into the storm layer and the drowned world below. Land is shrinking, so claiming and holding isles is what everyone fights over.

**Sinking can be slowed, never stopped.** Rootborn tending of the ancient trees slows an isle's sinking, but once an isle starts to fall it can't be saved.

**New isles are born.** Rarely, a new isle rises out of the storm layer. An isle's birth is a world event: unclaimed land, untouched resource nodes and a race to settle and hold it.

**The storm below is alive.** The storm layer is slowly rising. It sends creatures up onto the isles and pulls isles down. It never wins and is never defeated: it is the constant pressure that makes the peoples need each other, and the reason the Tether Accord holds.

## Tone and technology

Pure high fantasy: swords, bows, spells and craft. No guns or engines. Ships are wooden sailships carried by enchanted sails and wind currents. They are common and player-crafted, as the [economy](../economy/README.md) assumes.

## The Tether Accord

A fragile trade pact between all five peoples, signed after a great sinking that nearly broke every people at once. It keeps the sky lanes open and the towns neutral. Under it, old grudges still run deep, and open-world PvP lives in the gaps between Accord towns.

## Homelands

| People | Homeland | Role in the world |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rootborn** | **The Rootholds**: caverns in the roots beneath the oldest isles | Believe roots slow the sinking; tend the ancient trees |
| **Veiled Ones** | None now. Their city, **Vael**, sank generations ago | Wander in **oath-caravans**: fleets of Veiled ships that sail the Accord lanes from town to town. The masks are a mourning vow worn until Vael is raised |
| **Mossbacks** | **The Garden Isles**: slow, terraced farmland isles | Keepers of the Ledger, the record of every isle that has sunk |
| **Skerrin** | **The Crag Spires**: sheer cliff isles | Miners and climbers who dig into the isles for stone and ore |
| **Rillfolk** | **The Glimmer Lagoons**: lake isles with glowing water | Traders between all peoples, and the boldest divers into the drowned world. Anyone can dive, but the Rillfolk know it best |

## Old grudges and ties

- **Rootborn vs Skerrin:** the Rootborn say Skerrin mining weakens isles and hastens the sinking. The Skerrin call that superstition.
- **Veiled vs Rillfolk:** Rillfolk divers bring up relics from the drowned world. The Veiled see that as robbing the dead of Vael.
- **Veiled vs Mossbacks:** the Mossback Ledger records that Vael sank because of a broken oath. The Veiled say the Ledger lies.
- **Rootborn and Mossbacks:** old allies who tend the land together.
- **Skerrin and Rillfolk:** trade partners, ore for goods and relics.

## The First Shard

Map: [`worlds/first-shard.world.json`](../../worlds/first-shard.world.json), editable in the world editor demo.

- **Accord Hall** sits at the centre. Four Accord towns (**Oathgate**, **Tetherport**, **Windmarket**, **Lanterncross**) sit between the homelands, joined by the clockwise **Accord Ring** wind lane, with spokes to the hub.
- **Homelands**: the Rootholds in the north, the Garden Isles in the east, the Glimmer Lagoons in the south (lowest, nearest the storm) and the Crag Spires in the west (highest). Each has a shrine isle bridged to three smaller isles and a lane from the nearest town.
- **Neighbours follow the ties**: Rootborn sit next to their Mossback allies, and Skerrin next to their Rillfolk trade partners. The Rootholds and Crag Spires share the contested northwest frontier.
- **Wild rim**: **Grudgewood**, an ancient tree on rich ore on the northwest frontier, between the Rootholds and the Crag Spires and beyond Oathgate; **Vael's Shadow**, ruins above where Vael sank; **Ashfall** and **Wreckrest** are sinking. Three wild currents (**The Long Gale**, **Drowned Drift**, **Grudge Wind**) are fast shortcuts through open-PvP sky.
- The Veiled have no homeland isle; their oath-caravans, fleets of Veiled ships, travel the Accord lanes and stop at every town.

## Gameplay hooks

- **Isle births** are rare world events: the whole shard races to chart, settle and contest the new land.
- **Sinking isles** can be a live event: an isle's last days, with evacuation, salvage and a race for its resources.
- **Drowned-world dives** are high-risk, high-reward expeditions for old-world relics, using the Salvage skill. Any player can dive; the Rillfolk are the most experienced divers and the lore experts.
- **Grudges** give PvP its flavour without hard factions; the Accord towns are the safe havens.

## Time and seasons

The world has a day and night cycle and four seasons.

## Open questions

- What the storm is: a force of nature, something sleeping, or the old world's doing? Players may never fully learn.
- How storm surges play out: creature waves, faster sinking, blocked wind lanes.
- Is Vael ever raised? That could be a long-term world event.
