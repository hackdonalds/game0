# Architecture

> Draft, under discussion. Concept sheets in `art/concept/architecture/`.

## Core idea: architecture answers the sinking

Every people faces the same question, how to build on land that is falling, and answers it differently.

| | Galeborne: build to leave | Anchorkin: build to stay | Rootwardens: grow, don't build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silhouette | Vertical: tall narrow towers, spires, sail arcs | Horizontal: squat, broad, stepped, battered walls | Round: domes, mounds, root arches, half-buried |
| Structure | Tension: ropes, struts, cantilevers over the edge | Mass: thick walls, heavy arches, forged chain as structure | Growth: buttress roots, woven living walls, moss roofs |
| Materials | Pale timber, cream sailcloth, whitewashed stone | Dark basalt and slate with teal crystal veins, black iron | Bark, root wood, moss, lichen, flowers |
| Meets the isle | Overhangs the cliff: perches, docks, landing platforms | Chained down the cliff and underside | Inside the isle: root caverns and the underside root mass |
| Doors | Tall and narrow | Huge | Low and round |
| Night light | Gold beacons and wind chimes | Teal crystal veins | Amber windows, teal fungus lanterns |
| Signature | Feather and sail motifs, weathervanes | Ledger inscription bands, oath-marks on doorposts | Flowers growing from the structure |

**Accord towns** are where the three meet: Anchorkin stone base, Rootwarden deadwood frames, Galeborne awnings and sails, terracotta roofs and gothic spires as in the approved market-town style test.

**The old world** left ruins in a fourth language nobody understands: precise, geometric, too perfect, pale stone with dead teal channels.

## Scale by role

Dwellings are humble and charming, civic buildings heroic (about 3× scale), landmarks monumental. The contrast is what makes the world feel epic.

## Quality rules

1. Silhouette first: every building reads as a black shape at 100 m.
2. No perfect geometry: lean, sag, uneven stones, chamfers, worn edges. Exaggerate big forms (oversized roofs, chunky doors).
3. Painted textures on shared trim sheets per people, not flat vertex colour.
4. History on every building: repairs, creeping moss, carvings, tilt on sinking isles.
5. Props are miniature architecture in their people's shape language and materials.
