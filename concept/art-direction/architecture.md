## Core idea: architecture answers the sinking

Every people faces the same question, how to build on land that is falling, and answers it differently. The Accord towns are where the peoples' styles meet, and the old world left ruins in a language nobody understands. Each style's summary, concept sheets and kit are on its [architecture entry](../entries/architecture/); this table is the one place its traits are written.

## Scale by role

Dwellings are humble and charming, civic buildings heroic (about 3× scale), landmarks monumental. The contrast is what makes the world feel epic.

## Quality rules

1. Silhouette first: every building reads as a black shape at 100 m.
2. No perfect geometry: lean, sag, uneven stones, chamfers, worn edges. Exaggerate big forms (oversized roofs, chunky doors).
3. Painted textures on shared trim sheets per people, not flat vertex colour.
4. History on every building: repairs, creeping moss, carvings, tilt on sinking isles.
5. Props are miniature architecture in their people's shape language and materials.
